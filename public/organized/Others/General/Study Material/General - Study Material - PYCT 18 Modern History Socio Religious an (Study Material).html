Solution:

The correct answer is Mahatma Gandhi.

  • Harijan Sevak Sangh was organized by Mahatma Gandhi in 1932 as a part of his constructive program for the removal of untouchability.

Key Points

  • Its parent organization was the All India Anti Untouchability League founded by Mahatma Gandhi earlier on 30 September 1932. 
  • This was later renamed Harijan Sevak Sangh. 
  • Its first president was Ghanshyam Das Birla and the secretary was Amritlal Takkar.
  •  It still exists as a non – governmental organization working for the welfare of Harijan or Dalit people and upliftment of the Depressed Class of India.

Key Points

  •  महात्मा गांधी द्वारा पहले इसका मूल संगठन 30 सितंबर 1932 को स्थापित अखिल भारतीय विरोधी अस्पृश्यता लीग था।

Q2

In which of the following cities was the Veda Samaj established in 1864?

1864 में निम्नलिखित में से किस शहर में वेद समाज की स्थापना हुई थी?

Solution:

The correct answer is Madras(Chennai).

Key Points

  • Veda Samaj was established by Keshab Chandra Sen and K. Sridharalu Naidu.
  • Established in Madras (Chennai) in 1864.
  • The Veda Samaj was inspired by the Brahmo Samaj.
  • It worked to abolish caste distinctions and promote widow remarriage and women's education.
  • Its members believed in one God. 
  • They condemned the superstitions and rituals of orthodox Hinduism.
  • An important ideology of the members of Veda Samaj was regarding marriage and the funeral rituals as matters of routine, destitute of all religious significance.

Additional Information

  • Brahmo Samaj:
    • It was started at Calcutta on 20 August in 1828 by Raja Ram Mohan Roy.
    • It was a monotheistic sect of Hinduism.
    • Brahmo samaj had no faith in any scripture as an authority.
    • It rejects incarnation.
    • It was against caste restrictions and propounded a mix of west-east religious qualities.
    • It denounced polytheism and idol worship opposed idolatry.
    • It denied the need for a priestly class for interpreting religious texts.
    • Brahmo Samaj spread the religious treasure and teachings of Vedas but never claimed that Vedas are infallible.

Q3

 Mahadev Govind Ranade was primarily associated with the:
महादेव गोविंद रानाडे मुख्य रूप से किससे जुड़े थे?

Prarthana Samaj
Brahmo Samaj 
आर्य समाज 
साधारण ब्रह्म समाज 
प्रार्थना समाज
ब्रह्म समाज 
Solution:

The correct answer is Prarthana Samaj.

Key Points

  • Prarthana Samaj:
    • The Prarthana Samaj was founded in 1867.
    • It was founded by Atmaram Pandurang.
    • Prarthana Samaj played a very important role in the Maharashtra renaissance.
    • It focused on religious and social reform in Maharashtra.
    • The mouthpiece called 'Subodh-Patrika' was run by the Prarthana Samaj.
    • Veeresalingam Pantulu was the Telugu reformer who encouraged the Prarthana Samaj in South India.

Additional Information

  • Arya Samaj was started by Dayanand Saraswati in 1875.
    • The first unit was set up in Bombay (Mumbai) and the HQs were set up in Lahore (Pakistan).
    • Swami Vrijanand was the teacher of Dayanand Saraswati.
    • Arya Samaj encouraged intercaste marriage and widow remarriages.
    • D.A.V (Dayanand Anglo Viswavidyalaya) colleges were established by the Arya Samaj.
    • The samaj got divided on the issue of the curriculum in the colleges.
    • One group favored English education but the other group favoured Sanskrit and Vedic philosophy.
    • Later the issue of Vegetarian and non-vegetarians became a bone of contention among the follower of the Samaj.
    • The samaj got divided in the year 1893.
  • Brahmo Samaj was founded by Raja Ram Mohan Roy in the year 1828.
    • The Brahmo Samaj was the first important organization of religious reforms.
    • The greatest achievement in the field of social reform was the abolition of Sati in 1829.
    • The two most important leaders of the Brahmo Samaj were Debendranath Tagore and Keshub Chandra Sen.  
    • Brahmo Samaj advocates the existence of an ‘Infinite Singularity.’ 
    • The Brahmo Samaj got divided into two categories – ‘Adi Brahmo Samaj’ and ‘Sadharan Brahmo Samaj.
    • As the Brahmo Sabha started to fall, Debendranath Tagore came up with Tattvaranjini Sabha, which later came to be known as ‘Tattvabodhini Sabha.’ 
    • After the controversy of the underage marriage of Keshub Chandra Sen's daughter, the Special Marriage Act of 1872 was enacted to set the minimum age of 14 years for the marriage of girls. 
      • All the Bramho Marriages were thereafter solemnised under the law.

    • आर्य समाज द्वारा D.A.V. (दयानंद एंग्लो विश्वविद्यालय) महाविद्यालयों की स्थापना की गई थी।
    • कॉलेजों में पाठ्यक्रम के मुद्दे पर समाज विभाजित हो गया।

    • 1893 में समाज का विभाजन हो गया।

    • ब्रह्म समाज के दो सबसे महत्वपूर्ण नेता देवेंद्रनाथ टैगोर और केशव चंद्र सेन थे।

    • जैसे ही ब्रह्म सभा का पतन शुरू हुआ, देबेंद्रनाथ टैगोर ने तत्वरंजिनी सभा की स्थापना की, जिसे बाद में 'तत्वबोधिनी सभा' के रूप में जाना जाने लगा।

Key Points

  • Satyashodhak Samaj (or the Truth-seekers' Society) was a social reform group founded by Jyotiba Phule in Pune, Maharashtra, on 24 September 1873.
  • Its mission was to educate and increase social rights and political access for the less
  • The Satyashodhak Samaj focused especially on women, Shudras, and Dalits, in Maharashtra.
  • Jyotiba's wife 
  • The organization attracted individuals of all castes, including many groups like Brahmans, Muslims, lawyers, merchants, peasants, land-owners, agricultural laborers, Rajputs, untouchables, etc. 
  • Phule thought that this Samaj could uplift disadvantaged communities through collective action and organized movement, and the first step to doing so was to achieve the education of low caste in the country.
  • The Samaj also published the "Deenbandhu" newspaper from 1877 to 1897.

    • It has worked to further female education and inter caste marriage, has built missions, orphanages, and homes for widows, has established a network of schools and colleges; and has undertaken famine relief and medical work. 
    • From its beginning, it was an important factor in the growth of Indian nationalism. 
  • Brahmo Samaj:-
    • It is an atheistic movement within Hinduism, founded in Kolkata in 1828 by Ram Mohan Roy. 
    • It does not accept the authority of the Vedas, has no faith in avatars (incarnations), and does not insist on belief in karma (causal effects of past deeds) or samsara (the process of death and rebirth).
    •  It discards Hindu rituals and denounces polytheism, image worship, and the caste system. 

सही उत्तर स्वामी विवेकानंद - रामकृष्ण मिशन है।

प्रमुख बिंदु

  • रामकृष्ण मिशन 1 मई 1897 को स्वामी विवेकानंद द्वारा स्थापित एक भारतीय सामाजिक-धार्मिक संगठन है।
  • इसकी स्थापना रामकृष्ण परमहंस के आदर्शों के प्रचार-प्रसार के लिए की गई थी।
  • यह स्वास्थ्य देखभाल, प्रारंभिक और उच्च शिक्षा, जनजातीय कल्याण और संस्कृति के क्षेत्र में अनुसंधान करता है।

अतिरिक्त जानकारी

  • आर्य समाज :-
    • यह दयानंद सरस्वती द्वारा 1875 में स्थापित आधुनिक हिंदू धर्म के नोबल्स जोरदार सुधार आंदोलन का समाज है।
    • उद्देश्य: - वेदों, प्राचीनतम हिंदू शास्त्रों को प्रकट सत्य के रूप में पुनः स्थापित करना।
    • आर्य समाज मूर्तियों (छवियों) की पूजा, पशु बलि, श्राद्ध (पूर्वजों की ओर से अनुष्ठान), योग्यता, अस्पृश्यता, बाल विवाह, तीर्थयात्रा, पुरोहित शिल्प और मंदिर के प्रसाद के बजाय जन्म के आधार पर जाति का विरोध करता है।
    • इसने महिला शिक्षा और अंतरजातीय विवाह को आगे बढ़ाने के लिए काम किया है, विधवाओं के लिए मिशन, अनाथालय और घर बनाए हैं, स्कूलों और कॉलेजों का एक नेटवर्क स्थापित किया है; और अकाल राहत और चिकित्सा कार्य किया है।
    • अपनी शुरुआत से ही, यह भारतीय राष्ट्रवाद के विकास में एक महत्वपूर्ण कारक था।
  • ब्रह्म समाज:-
    • यह हिंदू धर्म के भीतर एक नास्तिक आंदोलन है , जिसकी स्थापना 1828 में राम मोहन राय ने कोलकाता में की थी।
    • यह वेदों के अधिकार को स्वीकार नहीं करता है, अवतारों (अवतार) में कोई विश्वास नहीं करता है, और कर्म (पिछले कर्मों के कारण प्रभाव) या संसार (मृत्यु और पुनर्जन्म की प्रक्रिया) में विश्वास पर जोर नहीं देता है।
    • यह हिंदू रीति-रिवाजों को खारिज करता है और बहुदेववाद, भगवान की छवि पूजा और जाति व्यवस्था की निंदा करता है।
