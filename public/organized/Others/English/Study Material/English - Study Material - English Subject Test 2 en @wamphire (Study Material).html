Q3

Select the correct passive voice of the given sentence.

She is making a beautiful beaded curtain.

A beautiful beaded curtain was being made by her.
A beautiful beaded curtain is being made by her.
A beautiful beaded curtain is made by her.
A beautiful beaded curtain was made by her.
Solution:

The correct answer is 'A beautiful beaded curtain is being made by her.'

Key Points

  • The given sentence is in the Active Voice. As per the given question we have to change the sentence into Passive Form. 
  • The given sentence is an example of 'present continuous'. 
  • The structure of these kinds of sentences is:
    • Subject + is/am/are + V1 + ing + Object. (Active Voice)
    • Subject(Objective Case) + is/am/are + being+ V3 + by + Object (subjective case). (Passive Voice) 
  • Example:
    • He is riding a bike. (Active Voice)
    • A bike is being ridden by him. (Passive Voice)
  • The subject of the given sentence is 'She' and the object of the given sentence is 'A beautiful beaded curtain'. 
  • The subject will be put in place of the object and the object will be put in place of the subject. 
  • Here, 'is making' will be changed into 'is being made'. 
  • The preposition 'by' will be used in the end.
     

Correct Sentence: A beautiful beaded curtain is being made by her.

Q5

Sentences of a paragraph are given below in jumbled order. Arrange the sentences in the correct order to form a meaningful and coherent paragraph.

A. Firstly, it provides air for respiration, serves the sense of smell and conditions the air by filtering, warming and moistening it.

B. The nose is the prominent structure between the eyes that serves as the entrance to the respiratory tract.

C. For inhalation it has two cavities, separated from one another by a wall of cartilage called the septum.

D. Apart from filtering the air, it also cleans itself of foreign debris extracted from inhalations.

BADC
ACDB
DBCA
BACD
Solution:

The correct answer is 'BADC'.

Key Points

  • The given question is an example of a Para Jumble.
  • We know that the first statement of a para jumbled question is usually an independent general statement, a noun, a universal fact, starting of an incident, or it begins with 'most' or 'once'.
    • Sentence B will be the starting line as it contains the main idea of the para jumble i.e. nose is the prominent structure of the respiratory tract.
    • However, Sentence A states that it provides air for respiration, serves the sense of smell, and conditions the air by filtering, warming, and moistening it.
    • So, A will follow B.
    • Further, Sentence D states that apart from filtering the air, it also cleans itself of foreign debris extracted from Inhalations and sentence C says that for inhalation it has two cavities.
    • So, C will follow D. 
    • Thus, the correct sequence is:
      • B: The nose is the prominent structure between the eyes that serves as the entrance to the respiratory tract.
      • A: Firstly, it provides air for respiration, serves the sense of smell and conditions the air by filtering, warming and moistening it.
      • D: Apart from filtering the air, it also cleans itself of foreign debris extracted from inhalations.
      • C: For inhalation it has two cavities, separated from one another by a wall of cartilage called the septum.
         

Correct Sequence: The nose is the prominent structure between the eyes that serves as the entrance to the respiratory tract. Firstly, it provides air for respiration, serves the sense of smell and conditions the air by filtering, warming and moistening it. Apart from filtering the air, it also cleans itself of foreign debris extracted from inhalations. For inhalation it has two cavities, separated from one another by a wall of cartilage called the septum.

Q12

Fill in the blank with the correct option.

The government ______ provide health care for everybody.

must
could
can
need
Solution:

The most suitable modal verb is must.

Key Points

  • ​An auxiliary verb is a verb that adds functional or grammatical meaning to the clause in which it occurs, so as to express tense, aspect, modality, voice, emphasis, etc.
  • A modal or a modal auxiliary is a word such as 'can' or 'would' which is used with the main verb (the 1st form of the verb) to express ideas such as possibility, intention, or necessity.
  • 'Must' expresses strong obligation and necessity.
  • Example:
    • I have taken my breakfast. (have- auxiliary verb)
    • One should obey one's elders. (should- modal)
  • Since an obligation is given in the question, the modal verb 'must' will be used in the blank part of the sentence.
     

Correct Sentence: The government must provide health care for everybody.

​Additional Information

  • If should is used after lest, it indicates a negative purpose.
  • Example:
    • Hurry up lest you should miss the bus.
